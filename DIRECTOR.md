# 🎬 DIRECTOR.MD — THE EDITORIAL DIRECTORIAL CONTRACT

## 1. Core Creative Philosophy
A viral social video (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok) is NOT a slide deck with voiceover. It is a **high-retention cinematic narrative**.

The pipeline strictly enforces:
$$\mathbf{Narration} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Meaning} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Visual \ Metaphor} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Shot \ Primitive} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Camera \ Choreography} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Sound \ Design}$$

Never:
$$\text{Narration} \longrightarrow \text{Card Box} \longrightarrow \text{Duplicate Spoken Text as Headline} \longrightarrow \text{Generic Zoom}$$

---

## 2. The 5-Beat Narrative Arc (Strict 30–40 Seconds)
Every script planned by the AI Director must follow this emotional retention curve:

```
Retention %
100% ┌───\ (Beat 1: Hook)
 90% │    \______ (Beat 2: Context & Problem)
 80% │           \_______ (Beat 3: Escalation / The Hidden Mechanism)
 85% │                   \________/^^^\ (Beat 4: Climax / Shocking Number)
 95% │                                 \______ (Beat 5: Payoff & Seamless Loop)
     └─────────────────────────────────────────────── Time (0s -> 35s)
```

1. **Beat 1: The Pattern Interrupt (0.0s – 2.5s)**
   - Must force the thumb to stop within 400ms.
   - Banned: *"In this video...", "Did you know that...", "Today we will talk about..."*
   - Allowed: Provocative question, shocking evidence, kinetic single-word impact, or instant mystery.
2. **Beat 2: The Context / Tension (2.5s – 8.0s)**
   - Introduce the stakes. Why does this matter? What went wrong?
   - Shot style: Archival documentary, historical map, or dramatic problem establishment.
3. **Beat 3: The Escalation / The Hidden Mechanism (8.0s – 18.0s)**
   - How it actually works. The secret move, the unseen algorithm, the financial crisis, the military tactic.
   - Shot style: Data crash/surge, pan-and-scan blueprint, split editorial comparison.
4. **Beat 4: The Climax / Impact Revelation (18.0s – 26.0s)**
   - The turning point. The massive valuation, the total collapse, the psychological trap.
   - Shot style: Massive glowing metric hero with camera shake and bass drop.
5. **Beat 5: The Payoff & Seamless Loop (26.0s – 35.0s)**
   - Conclude with a memorable takeaway that connects grammatically into the first sentence so the video loops endlessly.

## 3. Strict Rules on Typography & Text Overlap
1. **Never Transcribe Spoken Sentences into Headlines:**
   - If voiceover says: *"The company lost ten billion dollars in an afternoon"*, the headline MUST NOT say *"THE COMPANY LOST TEN BILLION DOLLARS"*.
   - Instead, the headline can be an evidence label: `[ OCT 24, 2008 ]` or simply the massive number `-$10,000,000,000`.
2. **Captions Own The Narration:**
   - Spoken words are handled exclusively by the dynamic lower-third Hormozi captions.
   - The top 75% of the frame is reserved exclusively for visual evidence, photography, charts, and diagrams.
3. **Safe Zones:**
   - Top Margin: Y: 0 – 260px (Reserved for status/category tags or clean space).
   - Bottom Danger Zone: Y: 1650 – 1920px (Reserved for platform UI like usernames, captions, and buttons).
   - Active Staging: Y: 260 – 1500px.

## 4. Multi-Track Audio Contract
Every shot MUST define:
- `sfx`: One of `['whoosh', 'impact_boom', 'sub_drop', 'paper_slam', 'cash_register', 'click', 'none']`
- `sfxDelayFrames`: Frame offset when the sound effect triggers (usually 0 to 6 frames).
- `musicMood`: One of `['dark_synth', 'cinematic_orchestral', 'lofi_chill', 'high_tension']`
