# Visual Style & Design Token Standards

## 1. Liquid Glass Design System (Apple Pro Aesthetic)
- **Glass Card Background**: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` to `rgba(8, 12, 22, 0.92)`
- **Backdrop Filter**: `blur(28px) saturate(180%)`
- **Specular Border**: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`
- **Surface Elevation Shadow**: `0 30px 80px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 40px {accentColor}25`
- **Spring Physics Curves**: `mass: 0.6`, `stiffness: 180`, `damping: 15`

---

## 2. Niche Visual Profiles & Color Schemes

### 1. Finance & Wealth
- **Primary / Accent**: `#10b981` (Emerald Mint) & `#f59e0b` (Gold)
- **Background**: Deep Forest / Obsidian (`#041a10` to `#010804`)
- **Key Visuals**: Animated line charts, data crash drops, leaked audit memos, glowing currency metrics.

### 2. Tech, AI & Systems
- **Primary / Accent**: `#38bdf8` (Electric Cyan) & `#a855f7` (Neon Purple)
- **Background**: Deep Obsidian (`#030712` to `#010206`)
- **Key Visuals**: Cyber grids, terminal windows, code IDE windows, metric counters.

### 3. Business, Startups & Scandals
- **Primary / Accent**: `#f59e0b` (Amber Gold) & `#3b82f6` (Cobalt) / `#f43f5e` (Crimson)
- **Background**: Slate Navy (`#0a1120` to `#04070d`)
- **Key Visuals**: Leaked executive documents, before/after split screens, notification pops.

### 4. History, Mysteries & Documentaries
- **Primary / Accent**: `#d97706` (Antique Amber) & `#fbbf24` (Parchment Gold)
- **Background**: Warm Bronze / Charcoal (`#1a1008` to `#080402`)
- **Key Visuals**: Authentic archival photographs, polaroids with washi tape, editorial serif quote cards.

### 5. Science & Deep Space
- **Primary / Accent**: `#06b6d4` (Starlight Teal) & `#8b5cf6` (Cosmic Violet)
- **Background**: Deep Space Indigo (`#060814` to `#010205`)
- **Key Visuals**: Macro photography, glowing radar charts, telemetry stats.

## 3. Typography Scale & Font Pairing
- **Editorial Headlines**: `'Playfair Display'`, `'Cinzel'`, Georgia (Weight: 900, Line-Height: 1.15)
- **Modern Sans Headlines & Labels**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans'`, `'Outfit'`, `'Inter Tight'` (Weight: 800-900)
- **Evidence / Quote Passage**: `'Newsreader'`, Georgia (Weight: 600 Italic)
- **Subtitles (Captions)**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans'`, All-Caps (Weight: 800, Size: 32px-40px, Active word scale: 1.06x with colored glow)
